Note on model performance — an R² of 0.998 reflects that overall AQI is calculated as the maximum of its pollutant sub-indices (EPA methodology), so PM2.5 — the dominant sub-index in this dataset — largely determines the target by construction. This makes the regression task close to deterministic rather than a hard real-world prediction problem; it's reported here for transparency alongside the feature-importance breakdown. Latitude/longitude were intentionally excluded from the model since AQI is chemistry-based, not location-based — included here for geographic distribution only.
